Pong

How to play

First to 5 points wins. The ball speeds up with every paddle hit, and where it strikes the paddle sets its angle.

  • Keyboard (1 player): W/S or ↑/↓ move your paddle.
  • Keyboard (2 players): W/S for the left paddle, ↑/↓ for the right.
  • Touch or mouse: drag on the court. The left half moves the left paddle, the right half moves the right paddle in 2 player mode.
  • 1 / 2 start a 1 or 2 player match, P or Esc pauses, R restarts.

Motion effects (ball trail, screen shake) are turned off when your system asks for reduced motion.
